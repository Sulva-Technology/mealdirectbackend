# MealDirect v2 — Roadmap

**Date:** 2026-07-25
**Status:** Agreed sequencing. Each slice gets its own design spec before implementation.
**Release model:** single v2 cutover — all slices land on a long-lived branch, one migration set, one deploy.

## Why a roadmap

The v2 brief covers six independent subsystems. They are specced and built one at a time; this
document records what they are, how they depend on each other, and the order they land in.

## Slices

| ID  | Slice                                       | Summary                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Depends on |
| --- | ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| A   | Wallet + Paystack dedicated account         | Per-customer wallet with a Paystack dedicated virtual account (DVA). Funding by bank transfer, paying orders from balance, refunds to balance. Direct Paystack checkout remains available per order.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | —          |
| B+C | Fee model rework **with timing incentives** | Customer delivery fee rises to ₦300 (₦400 point delivery); the split becomes admin-configurable (platform / rider / vendor). Rider ₦100 on-time else ₦50; vendor ₦50 if food ready ≥10 min early (rider-confirmed at pickup) else ₦0; point surcharge ₦50 to the fulfiller. **Every forfeit falls through to the platform.** C is merged into B because the shares are inherently timing-conditional — see the [design](2026-07-28-fee-model-timing-design.md). | —          |
| D   | Order status collapse                       | Reduce the customer-visible lifecycle to four states: `paid`, `ready`, `out_for_delivery`, `delivered`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | C          |
| E   | Vendor and rider payouts via Paystack       | Provision transfer recipients for vendors, enable automated transfers, reconcile payouts. Largely built already in `src/modules/settlements/payout.service.ts` behind `PAYOUTS_ENABLED`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | B          |
| F   | Eating-habit tracking                       | Customer-facing analytics over completed orders: what they eat, how often, spend over time.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | D          |

## Build order

**A → B+C → D → E → F**, with A first by decision. B and C are now one slice (merged 2026-07-28).

B is the foundation for the money changes: D and E all read the numbers B makes configurable.
D is the most invasive migration (enum plus the transition matrix plus historical rows), so it
lands after the fee semantics it must encode are settled. F is last because it consumes the
delivered-order signal D defines.

## Current-state notes carried into v2

Facts established while scoping, worth not re-deriving:

- The delivery-fee split is **hardcoded**, not configurable: `orders_money_non_negative` asserts
  `platform_delivery_share_kobo = 7500 and fulfiller_delivery_share_kobo = 7500`, and
  `delivery_batches_earnings_formula` asserts `delivery_earnings_kobo = order_count * 7500`.
  Slice B must relax both constraints, not merely add columns.
- The vendor receives no share of the delivery fee today.
- `src/domain/order-status.ts` holds twelve statuses mirroring the `order_status` DB enum and
  `transition_order_status`. The DB is authoritative; both move together in slice D.
- Paystack transfers are already implemented (`payout.service.ts`, `/transferrecipient`,
  `/transfer`) and gated by `PAYOUTS_ENABLED`. Slice E is mostly enablement and vendor recipient
  provisioning, not new integration work.
- There is no settings table. Every operator-tunable value today is either an environment
  variable (`SERVICE_FEE_KOBO`, `MAX_ORDER_TOTAL_KOBO`, `LARGE_ORDER_SURCHARGE_BPS`) or a column
  on `vendors` / `campuses` / `campus_zones`. Slice A introduces a generic `platform_settings`
  table; slice B reuses it for the fee splits.
