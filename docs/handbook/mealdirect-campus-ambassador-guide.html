Welcome aboard

You are the face of MealDirect on your campus

Students won't trust an app until they trust the person who told them about it. That person is you. This guide gives you everything you need to explain MealDirect clearly and do the job well.

The short versionWhat you are signing up for

A Campus Ambassador is a student who grows MealDirect on their campus. You get students signed up with your referral code, help them place their first order and top up their wallet, and get them ordering again. You also pass on what students are saying to the campus team.

01
Part one

What is MealDirect?

MealDirect is a food-ordering app built only for campuses. Students order ahead for a set meal time, the vendor cooks everything at once, and one rider delivers the whole batch.

The problemCampus food is a daily headache

Long queues between lectures. Walking across campus in the heat. Delivery apps that charge too much, arrive late, or won't come past the gate. Vendors who either run out of food or end up throwing it away.

The fixScheduled batch delivery

Students order before a cut-off for a fixed meal time like Lunch at 1:00 pm. Every order for one vendor, one meal time and one zone becomes a single batch. The vendor cooks exactly that many plates, and one rider delivers all of them in one trip.

Part two · Know it cold

How it works for students

You'll be asked these questions many times. If you can explain this page from memory, you can handle almost any conversation.

The student journeyFrom signup to first meal

  1. Sign up and enter a referral code (yours)Email or phone, then verify the code they receive. The referral code must be entered during signup. See Part 4.
  2. Pick campus and usual locationTheir hostel or department. This sets their zone and which batch their orders join.
  3. Turn on notificationsThis is how they find out the food is ready and the rider is on the way.
  4. Choose the meal time first, then the foodPick the date and slot (e.g. Lunch · 1:00 pm). The app only shows vendors and items available for that slot.
  5. Build the plateQuantities, soup choice where needed, extras like spoons of rice, protein and drinks. They can add a short note ("no pepper").
  6. Choose where it's delivered, then payStandard drop point, or point delivery to a room or class for ₦100 more. Pay from the wallet or by card. An order only counts once it's paid.
The cut-off is strict

Each meal time closes a set time before delivery (usually 90 minutes for lunch). After that, students have to order for the next slot. An order left unpaid past the cut-off expires on its own. No food, no charge. Remind people before the cut-off.

The walletThe feature to show off

Every student gets a MealDirect wallet with their own bank account number (through Paystack). They transfer money to it from any Nigerian bank app and their balance goes up. No card, and no typing card details every time they order.

  • Top up: Wallet → copy account number → transfer
  • Pay: choose Wallet at checkout; the app shows any shortfall
  • Refunds: go straight back into the wallet
  • Ledger: every movement is listed, so it's easy to prove what happened

After payingThe four order states

paidThe vendor has the order on their list.
readyCooked and packed, waiting for the rider.
out_for_deliveryThe rider has the batch and is on the way.
deliveredHanded over. Reviews open now.

Batch group chat

Each batch has a chat with the other students on the same run, the vendor and the rider. Students are shown under nicknames, so no names or phone numbers are visible.

Hand-off code

The rider may ask for a hand-off code. Students should only show it once the food is in their hands.

Problems & refunds

Wrong, missing or cold food, or nothing delivered? They report it on the order. Approved refunds go to their wallet.

Your role as Campus Ambassador

Your goal is to get students ordering and keep them ordering. Every responsibility below supports that.

Get every student on your campus to their first order, and make that order go so well that they place a second one.

Your mission in one sentence

Get students signed up with your referral code entered at signup. Use hostels, departments, fellowships, clubs, sports teams and class group chats.

Part four · Read twice

Your referral code

Every MealDirect user has a personal referral code, including you. It's how your work is tracked. One rule decides whether a referral counts.

The golden rule: the code must be entered during signup

A referral code only works before the student finishes setting up their profile. Once their profile is complete, they can never add a code. If a friend signs up first and plans to add your code later, that referral is lost. Always get them to enter your code on the signup screen.

Step by stepGetting your code

  1. Create your own MealDirect accountUse the customer app like any student. You need to know the app well before you can teach it.
  2. Open your referral code in the appIt's unique to you and is created automatically.
  3. Put it everywhereWhatsApp status, bio, flyers, a QR code at your stand. Make it short and easy to remember.

Step by stepSigning up a friend

  1. Open the app on their phone with themSigning up together works much better than "I'll send you the link."
  2. Enter your code on the signup screenBefore they finish setting up their profile. Check it's correct.
  3. Set campus, location, notificationsTheir hostel or department, plus push notifications.
  4. Show them the walletPoint out their account number and how to transfer to it.
  5. Find tomorrow's meal time togetherShow them the slot and cut-off, so their first order isn't late.

Each person uses a code once

A student can redeem one referral code, one time. They can't change it later.

You can't refer yourself

Your own account can't use your code. Only real students count.

Paying users are what count

Fake or empty signups show up as referrals who never paid. They don't help you, and they get noticed.

Part seven · Non-negotiable

Code of conduct

Students trust MealDirect because they trust you. These rules protect them, the brand and you.

Always

  • Be honest about fees, times and cut-offs, even when it costs you a signup
  • Let students type their own details on their own phones
  • Send problems to the campus team the same day
  • Use only promo codes and materials the campus team gave you
  • Respect university rules on where you can promote and set up stands
  • Treat vendors and riders as teammates, because they are
  • Keep any student information you see private

Never

  • Take cash for an order. Every order is paid in the app
  • Ask for or handle anyone's password, OTP, PIN or card details
  • Ask for a student's hand-off code, or share your own
  • Make fake accounts or pay people to sign up and do nothing
  • Promise refunds, discounts or free food the campus team hasn't approved
  • Speak to the media or the university administration on MealDirect's behalf
  • Run down other vendors, riders or competitors in public
Money safety

If someone offers to "just pay you directly", or asks you to pay for them and they'll pay you back, say no. Point them to the app and their wallet. Report anything that looks like a scam to the campus team straight away. Any account caught gaming referrals will be removed from the programme.
